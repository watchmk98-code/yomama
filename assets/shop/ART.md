`shopkeeper-sprites-v2.png` uses the approved male shopkeeper concept as a likeness and style reference. Prompt: six registered 16-bit pixel-art frames in a 3 × 2 grid: neutral, half blink, closed blink, raised eyebrow, nod down, nod return.
