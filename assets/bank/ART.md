# Bank art

`bank-counter.png` is the scene used by the live Bank page. Created with built-in
ImageGen from the approved `bank-section-concept-v3.png`. The earlier concept
images and standalone character images are retained as design references.

Final generation prompt:

> Edit the supplied approved game bank screen into ONE standalone environment
> illustration asset: reproduce ONLY its central rectangular scene showing the
> stout friendly moustached banker in burgundy waistcoat and cream shirt, without
> hat or visor, standing behind his wood counter with his hand on a ledger,
> round brass vault door behind him and green banker lamps. Preserve character
> identity, expression, pose, costume and existing richly detailed warm amber
> painted pixel-inspired art. Fill a landscape 2:1 image edge to edge with this
> bank-counter scene. Remove ALL UI, headers, borders, numbers, words,
> typography, menus, buttons, navigation, and surrounding interface. No writing
> anywhere in the interior, on walls or book spines. This is a game background
> plate for real HTML controls outside the image. Render only the banker,
> counter and bank interior. No additional people.

`bank-black-background.png` is the new page background: a black/charcoal bank
vault hall with faint brass highlights, dark central floor, no people or text.
Generated with built-in ImageGen for the reference-led redesign. The contrast
keeps the live gold frames and green balances readable; it is not baked-in UI.

`bank-icons.svg` is an original, code-native icon sheet: cash bundle, classical
bank, seven-day calendar, deposit, withdrawal, exchange, lending, repayment,
history and help. It uses the Bank's mint and brass palette; action symbols
inherit the control color. No font/emoji substitution is required.
