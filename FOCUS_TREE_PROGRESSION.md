# Focus tree progression

The focus tree is an optional layer of permanent business improvements. It
does not unlock construction, replace business quests, award Prestige, or
require the currently disabled Research system. One focus runs at a time on
the existing class clock; teacher pauses freeze it.

## Pacing and requirements

Times are rounded up to a whole class tick. Owned-business checks use completed
buildings, not construction queues. Connected nodes require all solid links;
the Regional Business Network accepts either of its dashed incoming links.

| Secure the Harvest | 5 | Own Farm |
| Reliable Supply | 10 | Own Farm |
| Build a Local Brand | 10 | Own Roastery |
| Breakfast Regulars | 15 | Own Farm and Roastery; sign a regular buyer |
| Signature Experience | 20 | Roastery Production level 3 |
| Regional Business Network | 20 | Own Farm; complete either regional food route |
| Workshop Foundations | 20 | Own Workshop |
| Clean Power | 30 | Own Solar Co-op |
| Powered Industry | 30 | Own Workshop and Solar Co-op |
| Preserve the Surplus | 30 | Own Cannery |
| Distribution Network | 30 | Own Cannery |
| Regional Supplier | 45 | Own Cannery |
| Automated Packaging | 45 | Own Cannery and Workshop; unlock Preserves through existing business development |
| Smart Grid | 60 | Own North Grid Plant and Signal Relay Station |
| Shared Logistics | 60 | Own Freight Terminal |
| Digital Marketplace | 90 | Own Data Hub |
| Connected Economy | 120 | Complete the regional, freight, and digital routes |

These timings reflect current construction: Fish Stall 1 minute, Roastery
3 minutes, Workshop 20 minutes, Solar Co-op 30 minutes, Cannery 60 minutes,
Relay Station 3 hours, Freight Terminal 3.5 hours, and Data Hub 4 hours.
They are a pacing baseline, not a claim of playtested optimal balance.

## Rewards and boundaries

Production bonuses add percentage points of base speed rather than multiplying
all other bonuses; a normal production upgrade adds 25 points. Focus production
bonuses range from 5 to 10 points per node. Storage bonuses add together, reaching
+45% for Farm, +25% for other Food and Industry businesses, and +10% for Energy
after completing the entire tree. Walk-in demand reaches +15% for Roastery and
+5% elsewhere. Orders and regular buyers each have one +8% reward bonus.

The 1,000 YM regional voucher is awarded once and pays only for upgrades. The
earlier Roastery route can earn it without waiting for freight infrastructure.
No focus grants a locked recipe, creates a business, or pays construction costs.

Completed focuses remain earned across upgrades. Existing active timers keep
their stored deadlines and display their actual duration; new starts use the
shorter timings. Class saves migrate in place without a reset. The page layout
and non-scrollable board are unchanged by this progression update.
