# First Shift: The First 25 Minutes

The purple notice board is the player's guide across Build and Market. It gives
one goal, one reason, and one action at a time. The player uses real stock,
orders, upgrades, quests, and expansion; the guide does not grant cash or goods.

| Target time | Lesson | Completion signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2 min | See the Farm's stock and automatic production | Open Stock from the board |
| 2-6 min | Find a Farm order and read Have / need | Open a Farm order in Market |
| 6-9 min | Save goods if needed, then deliver | Server records the first completed order |
| 9-13 min | See the Cash reward and buy an upgrade | A Farm upgrade level rises |
| 13-18 min | Open the current Farm quest | View its real goal and reward |
| 18-22 min | Inspect the next business and its requirements | Open Expansion |
| 22-25 min | Practice choosing orders, quests, or expansion | Active play reaches 25 minutes |

These times are targets, not deadlines. Lessons advance from server state or
the player's own guide actions. A player who takes longer keeps the current
lesson. A player who reaches practice early gets contextual suggestions until
the 25-minute active-play mark. The clock counts visible, unpaused time on
Build and Market; leaving the tab or a teacher pause stops it.

## Board Rules

- Start automatically for a new Farm seat. Returning, developed seats keep the
  normal business notices. Progress is stored per seat in this browser.
- Prefer the starter tomato order. If it is gone, choose another Farm-only
  order. If there is none, explain that free rerolls can find one.
- On Market, point at the actual Save or Deliver button. On compact screens,
  switch to the relevant tab or order page before highlighting the target.
- During steps 1-7, reduce competing panels and metrics. Give the chosen Farm
  order visual priority, and reveal Upgrades and Expansion when their lessons
  arrive. Hiding the board immediately restores the full interface.
- Use the active quest system for the class. If quests are absent, continue
  directly to Expansion.
- During practice, prioritize a ready quest, an affordable business, active
  construction, and a newly opened second business.
- The close button hides the guide. The question-mark button reopens it. The
  first-shift progress survives page changes and refreshes on the same device.
- A class reset restarts the guide when the class clock returns to an earlier
  tick. Finishing returns Build and Market to their normal notices.

## Acceptance Check

A scripted fresh-seat walkthrough checks the Build-to-Market path, first
delivery, upgrade, quest, expansion, restoration after hiding the board, and
desktop/mobile layout. This verifies function and presentation, not whether a
new player understands them.

A new player should be able to say what to do next within 30 seconds of
joining, complete a delivery without reading a separate manual, understand
what Cash buys, and identify the next business goal. Test with players who
have never seen the game; observe where they hesitate and whether they need
to ask what a board action means.
